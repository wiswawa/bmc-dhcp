# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and patient. English and Chinese participation are equally welcome. Critique code and behavior without attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, impersonate or disclose private information about others.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, limit participation or remove access. They will explain moderation decisions when doing so does not expose private information or create further harm. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a ban.

Report an incident through the repository's private reporting channel once enabled, or contact a listed maintainer privately through their public profile. Do not post another person's private details in a public issue. Maintainers involved in an incident should recuse themselves from handling it.

This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.
